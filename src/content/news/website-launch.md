We are glad to welcome you to the new website of the Mark Ofosu-Ampadu Jnr (MO-AJ) Foundation.

This site is where we will share the stories of road accident victims and their families, news from our road safety campaigns and events, and the many ways you can stand with us: by giving, by volunteering, or simply by sharing a story.

If you or your family have been affected by a road crash and would like to share your story, or if you would like to partner with us, please [get in touch](/contact).

Thank you for being part of this journey.
