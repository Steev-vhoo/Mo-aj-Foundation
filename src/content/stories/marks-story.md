My beloved late brother, affectionately called "Junior", was a very lively, principled, ambitious, industrious, selfless and God-fearing gentleman. His bright light was snuffed out in the late hours of Saturday, 25th January 2020, on account of a road traffic accident.

The harrowing accounts and pictures of the scene that we heard and saw in the days that followed shocked our senses and deepened our grief. They brought home a hard truth: that our national response to road accidents, and to road safety as a whole, was inadequate, and may have contributed to the loss of our son, brother, uncle, cousin and friend, Mark.

## A year of grief, and of purpose

I cannot recount all that the family and Mark's loved ones have been through since his passing. It has been an incredibly trying time: a time of tears and sorrow, of repeated questions and self-reflection, of dismay and shock.

But it has also been a time of deep thinking, of family unity and bonding, of Christian triumph and strength, and of finding a purpose: to cause tangible change.

> The foundation seeks to put a human face to the road carnage in Ghana.

## Why the foundation

It is in this spirit that the Mark Ofosu-Ampadu Jnr Foundation was established: as a vehicle for road safety advocacy and awareness, and as a celebration of Mark's life through the ideals he held dear, including community engagement, educational excellence, financial independence, entrepreneurship and leadership.

We want to tell the stories of road accident victims and of the families who have lost loved ones, and to support those families to keep moving forward. We want to rally support to influence road policy and infrastructure where the decisions are made. Above all, we want to stop other families from suffering a loss like ours.

We know this is only the beginning. We invite everyone to join and support the Ofosu-Ampadu family and the MO-AJ Foundation on the journey ahead.

*The Ofosu-Ampadu family*
